## Hero

`images/predel-keychain.png` — оригинальная CGI-иллюстрация, созданная
встроенным инструментом imagegen для predel. PNG RGBA, 1024×1536,
прозрачный фон. Дата: 20 сентября 2026. Не является логотипом VK/Butter.
Изображение не содержит интерфейса, текста страницы или чужих материалов.

Финальный промпт:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: isolated central hero artwork for predel,
> a premium Russian presentation-making web app, visually compatible with a
> VK-blue and white editorial site. Create one photorealistic CGI product shot:
> a polished chrome keyring with a short heavy silver chain suspending three
> tactile chunky charms. Main charm: glossy electric blue (#0077FF) softly rounded
> square with a white lowercase letter 'p' recessed into its surface. Second charm:
> frosted translucent pale-blue horizontal rectangle with three white embossed
> horizontal lines, symbolizing a presentation slide. Third charm: a small
> mirror-chrome four-point sparkle. Group arranged with believable gravity,
> overlapping at different angles, delightful physical finish and crisp studio
> highlights. Perspective three-quarter front, entire object visible, compact
> vertical grouping with generous clear margins. Premium industrial design,
> playful but restrained. Genuine transparent alpha background, absolutely no
> backdrop or floor, no drop shadow beyond object contact shadows. No people,
> no text except the single lowercase 'p', no VK or Butter logos, no website UI,
> no frames, no extra props. Deliver a high resolution transparent PNG.
